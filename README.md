All 55 GTA VI trophies, cited guides, a platinum roadmap and the missables — a checklist tracker that saves in your browser, no network needed.

Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)'s "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## What it does

- Checklist of all 55 trophies (1 platinum / 2 gold / 6 silver / 46 bronze, 28 hidden), with names, descriptions and grades exactly as the official list
- Filter by category, grade and search; spoiler toggle (default ON)
- Detail panel per trophy with a guide in the entry's own words and named sources
- Platinum roadmap in 5 stages, missable grid (only 1 potentially missable trophy, honestly flagged as unconfirmed)
- Progress saved in localStorage, export/import as JSON

## Sources

Every guide is written in the entry's own words. Sources checked during the build: PowerPyx (trophy guide & roadmap, full page read), Push Square (trophy guide & roadmap, full page read), Exophase (official trophy list) and PSNProfiles. 181 claims cited, 55/55 trophies with 2+ sources. Five trophies are listed as currently unknown by PowerPyx; their guides say so honestly instead of guessing. Key facts: about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, no online trophies.
